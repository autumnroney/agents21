Privacy Notice

Last updated 2026-10-20

The short version

Viral Finder has no accounts and sets no cookies. We don't sell or share your data. We keep only what we need to run searches and to count how the site is used.

What we process

  • Your search. The keyword, platform and time range you choose are sent to our server and passed on to ScrapeCreators to fetch results. Shareable links put your search in the page address, so anyone you send a link to can see it.
  • Technical data. Like every website, our host receives your IP address, browser user agent and the requested address (which includes your search) when you load the page or search. We do not store these ourselves.
  • Usage events. To see whether the site works well, the page sends small events such as "search", "results shown", "load more", "video opened", "link copied" and "error". They contain the platform, time range, counts, timings and a random ID that lasts only for your browser tab. They do not contain your search text (only a length range such as "11-20 characters"), your IP address or your user agent. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, no events are sent.

Who processes it

  • Netlify hosts the site and runs our server functions. Its request and function logs are kept for the period of our Netlify plan's log retention.
  • ScrapeCreators receives your search keyword, platform and time range (not your IP address) to fetch public video data.
  • TikTok and YouTube serve the video thumbnails directly to your browser, so they receive your IP address when images load. We ask your browser not to send them the page address (no referrer).

How long we keep it

Search results are cached for about 5 minutes to save cost. Usage events and server logs are kept for no more than 90 days, or less if our hosting plan's log retention is shorter. A daily counter of how many searches the site made (with no personal data) is kept so we can cap costs.

Your choices

You can use Viral Finder without giving us any personal information. Turn on Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control in your browser to stop usage events. Video creators' names and public view counts shown in results come from the platforms; we don't store them or build profiles.
